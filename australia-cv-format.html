In Australia and New Zealand a CV is usually longer and more detailed than a US resume, with referees listed at the end.

Key rules for a Australia and NZ CV

  • Photo: No. Photos are not expected.
  • Length: Two to three pages is normal, longer than a US resume.
  • Personal details: Skip date of birth, marital status and photo. Citizenship or visa status helps if you need work rights.

Sections to include

Career profile, key skills, employment history, education, and referees (two names with contact details, or 'available on request').

Extra tips

  • Address any selection criteria in the advert with specific examples.
  • Use Australian or New Zealand spelling and A4 paper.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving out referees when the advert asks for them.
  • Cutting the CV to one page and dropping key achievements.
