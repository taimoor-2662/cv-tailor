Key rules for a Canada CV

  • Photo: No. Canadian employers do not expect a photo, and it can count against you.
  • Length: One to two pages; two pages is accepted for experienced professionals.
  • Personal details: Do not include date of birth, marital status, nationality or a photo. Mention work authorisation only if relevant (for example citizen, permanent resident or work permit).

Sections to include

Summary of qualifications, skills, work experience, education, and optional volunteer work or certifications.

Extra tips

  • List English and French ability clearly, especially for bilingual roles.
  • Use Canadian spelling such as colour and centre.

Common mistakes

  • Adding a photo or personal data.
  • Leaving out language ability for bilingual roles.
