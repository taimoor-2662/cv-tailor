Key rules for a Spanish and Portuguese CV

  • Photo: Optional but common. A professional photo is often included, particularly in Spain.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: Contact details and location; date of birth, nationality and driving licence are often listed but optional.

Sections to include

Personal data, professional experience, education, languages, IT skills, and additional training.

Extra tips

  • Show language levels with CEFR levels or clear descriptions.
  • Use Spanish or Portuguese as the CV language for local employers.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping language and IT skill levels.
  • Making the CV longer than two pages.
