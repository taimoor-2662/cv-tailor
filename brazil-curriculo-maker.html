In Brazil the CV is called a curriculo; choose Portuguese for Brazil and Spanish for most of Latin America as the CV language.

Key rules for a Brazilian and Latin American CV

  • Photo: Optional. Photos are less expected than in the past.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: Brazilian CVs often include city, age and sometimes marital status; these are optional. Never include ID numbers such as the CPF.

Sections to include

Objective or summary, professional experience, education, courses and certifications, and languages.

Extra tips

  • Courses and certifications carry weight in Brazil, so list them.
  • Choose Portuguese or Spanish as the CV language.

Common mistakes

  • Listing ID numbers.
  • Writing a vague objective like 'seeking a good opportunity'.
