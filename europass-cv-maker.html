Europass (EU) CV Format and Free CV Maker

Europass is the standard CV format promoted by the European Union, and employers across Europe recognise its structure. Our Europass-style layout follows the same sections, but it is not the official Europass tool.

Key rules for a Europass-style CV

  • Photo: Optional. The format has an optional photo field, but many employers prefer none, especially for ATS uploads.
  • Length: Usually two pages. Keep only the most relevant experience.
  • Personal details: Include your name, contact details and location. Date of birth, nationality and gender are optional; add them only if the employer asks.

Sections to include

Personal information, work experience, education and training, language skills (with CEFR levels A1 to C2), digital skills, and other skills or certificates.

Extra tips

  • List experience and education in reverse chronological order.
  • Describe languages with CEFR levels such as B2 or C1.

Common mistakes

  • Writing 'fluent' without a CEFR level.
  • Keeping empty template fields instead of removing sections you do not need.
