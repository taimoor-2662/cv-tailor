Key rules for a Italian CV

  • Photo: Optional. Photos are accepted but not required.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: Contact details and location are enough. Date of birth and nationality are commonly listed but optional.

Sections to include

Personal data, work experience, education and training, language skills, digital skills, and a data-protection consent line.

Extra tips

  • End with a standard authorisation to process personal data under the EU GDPR; it is commonly included on Italian CVs.
  • Write in Italian for Italian-speaking employers.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting the data-processing consent line.
  • Applying in English to a company that works in Italian.
