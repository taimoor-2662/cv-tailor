About Free AI CV Tailor

Free AI CV Tailor helps job seekers match their CV to each job. You upload your CV, add the job, choose a template and regional format, and download the result as PDF or Word. It also includes an ATS score checker, a cover letter writer and a design studio.

The AI can make mistakes. It is told to use only facts from your CV, and the site flags details it cannot find in your original, but you are responsible for checking the final CV before you send it.

The project is free and open source: view it on GitHub.
