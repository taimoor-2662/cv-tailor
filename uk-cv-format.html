Key rules for a UK CV

  • Photo: No. UK CVs normally do not include a photo unless the role is in acting, modelling or similar fields.
  • Length: Two pages is the usual maximum; one page is common for graduates and early-career applicants.
  • Personal details: Leave out date of birth, marital status and nationality. The Equality Act 2010 makes these details unnecessary. Mention your right to work only if it helps.

Sections to include

Personal statement, key skills, employment history, education and qualifications, and optionally interests. 'References available on request' is optional.

Extra tips

  • Use UK spelling (organised, programme).
  • Open with a three to four line personal statement tailored to the job.

Common mistakes

  • Including a photo, date of birth or marital status.
  • Going over two pages with older, irrelevant roles.
