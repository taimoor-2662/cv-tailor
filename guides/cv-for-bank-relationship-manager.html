How to Write a Bank Relationship Manager CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Banks look for sales and recovery numbers, plus careful documentation and compliance. Show targets, portfolio size and risk awareness. This guide shows what to put in a bank relationship manager CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Client relationships
  • Loan processing
  • Deposit products
  • Credit analysis
  • Field verification
  • Recovery follow-ups
  • Sales targets
  • Documentation

Example professional summary

Relationship manager with experience in lending, deposits and client recovery.

Example achievement bullets

  • Achieved monthly sales and recovery targets for 36 months
  • Processed loan applications including documentation and credit analysis
  • Conducted field visits to verify clients and evaluate businesses

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a bank relationship manager CV

  • Not giving portfolio size, number of clients or recovery rate.
  • Leaving out loan products and credit analysis experience.
  • Skipping compliance, KYC or documentation work.

Tips that work for this role

  • Put targets next to actual results where you can.
  • Mention field verification and client visits, which show hands-on credit judgement.
