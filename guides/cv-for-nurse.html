How to Write a Nurse CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a nurse CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for NurseCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Patient care
  • Vital signs monitoring
  • Medication administration
  • Electronic health records
  • Infection control
  • Teamwork
  • Emergency response
  • Patient education

Example professional summary

Compassionate nurse with strong clinical skills and a calm approach in emergencies.

Example achievement bullets

  • Cared for 12 patients per shift in a busy ward
  • Maintained 100% accuracy in medication administration records
  • Trained 6 new nurses on ward procedures

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
