How to Write a Nurse CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Healthcare employers screen for licences first. Your registration, clinical areas and patient numbers should be easy to spot. This guide shows what to put in a nurse CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Patient care
  • Vital signs monitoring
  • Medication administration
  • Electronic health records
  • Infection control
  • Teamwork
  • Emergency response
  • Patient education

Example professional summary

Compassionate nurse with strong clinical skills and a calm approach in emergencies.

Example achievement bullets

  • Cared for 12 patients per shift in a busy ward
  • Maintained 100% accuracy in medication administration records
  • Trained 6 new nurses on ward procedures

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a nurse CV

  • Not listing your nursing licence or registration and its expiry date.
  • Leaving out the clinical settings you worked in, such as ICU or paediatrics.
  • Describing duties without patient ratios or shift volumes.

Tips that work for this role

  • List certifications such as BLS or ACLS with their dates.
  • Mention the electronic health record systems you know.
