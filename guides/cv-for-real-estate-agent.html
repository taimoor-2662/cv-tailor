How to Write a Real Estate Agent CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Property employers want closed deals, market knowledge and proper registration. Local expertise matters as much as sales figures. This guide shows what to put in a real estate agent CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Property sales
  • Client negotiation
  • Market analysis
  • Lead follow-up
  • Property viewings
  • CRM
  • Contracts and documentation
  • Local market knowledge

Example professional summary

Real estate professional with strong negotiation skills and deep knowledge of the local property market.

Example achievement bullets

  • Sold 25 properties worth over $8M in one year
  • Built a client database of 300+ buyers and investors
  • Shortened the average sales cycle by 15 days through faster follow-up

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a real estate agent CV

  • Leaving out your licence or registration details.
  • Listing 'sold properties' without values, number of units or timeframe.
  • Ignoring the areas, property types or developers you specialise in.

Tips that work for this role

  • State your territory and property types, such as off-plan, resale or rentals.
  • Add your lead-to-sale conversion or average days on market if you track it.
