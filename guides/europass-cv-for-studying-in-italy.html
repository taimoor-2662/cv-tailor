CV for Master's Applications in Italy and Europe

Universities and employers in Europe like clear, structured CVs.

  • Use a Europass-style layout: personal information, education, experience, skills, languages.
  • Add your language level (for example IELTS or CEFR levels).
  • List relevant coursework, projects and your thesis topic.
  • Keep it to 2 pages and use reverse-chronological order.
  • Include certificates, internships and volunteer work.
  • Match your CV to the course description and keep claims truthful.
