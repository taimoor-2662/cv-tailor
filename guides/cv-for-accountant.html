How to Write a Accountant CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a accountant CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for AccountantCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Bookkeeping
  • Financial reporting
  • Reconciliation
  • Tax preparation
  • QuickBooks
  • Excel
  • Accounts payable and receivable
  • Auditing

Example professional summary

Detail-focused accountant experienced in reporting, reconciliation and tax compliance.

Example achievement bullets

  • Prepared monthly financial statements for 12 clients with 100% on-time delivery
  • Reduced reconciliation errors by 30% by introducing a checklist
  • Managed accounts payable of over $1M annually

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
