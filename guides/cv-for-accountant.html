How to Write an Accountant CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Accuracy and compliance matter most. Employers look for your qualifications, the software you know and the size of the accounts you handled. This guide shows what to put in an accountant CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Bookkeeping
  • Financial reporting
  • Reconciliation
  • Tax preparation
  • QuickBooks
  • Excel
  • Accounts payable and receivable
  • Auditing

Example professional summary

Detail-focused accountant experienced in reporting, reconciliation and tax compliance.

Example achievement bullets

  • Prepared monthly financial statements for 12 clients with 100% on-time delivery
  • Reduced reconciliation errors by 30% by introducing a checklist
  • Managed accounts payable of over $1M annually

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in an accountant CV

  • Not listing your professional qualification or membership, such as ACCA, CPA or CA.
  • Skipping the accounting software, such as QuickBooks, SAP or Xero.
  • Describing 'bookkeeping' without the volume or value involved.

Tips that work for this role

  • Quantify budgets, ledgers or the number of clients you managed.
  • Mention audits and deadlines you met, and any errors you reduced.
