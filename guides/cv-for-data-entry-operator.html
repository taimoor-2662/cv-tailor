How to Write a Data Entry Operator CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Speed and accuracy are the two things employers check. Put both numbers near the top. This guide shows what to put in a data entry operator CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Typing speed
  • Accuracy
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Data verification
  • Confidentiality
  • Attention to detail
  • Database software
  • Time management

Example professional summary

Accurate data entry operator with fast typing and strong attention to detail.

Example achievement bullets

  • Entered 500+ records per day with 99.5% accuracy
  • Verified and corrected 3,000 records during a database migration
  • Typed at 65 WPM while meeting every daily deadline

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a data entry operator CV

  • Not stating your typing speed (words per minute) or accuracy rate.
  • Leaving out the software you used, such as Excel or database tools.
  • Forgetting to mention the confidentiality of the data you handled.

Tips that work for this role

  • Show volume: records per day or per week.
  • Mention any quality checks you did, such as double-entry verification.
