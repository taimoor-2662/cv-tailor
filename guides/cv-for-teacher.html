How to Write a Teacher CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a teacher CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for TeacherCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Lesson planning
  • Classroom management
  • Assessment
  • Curriculum development
  • Differentiated instruction
  • Parent communication
  • Edtech tools
  • Mentoring

Example professional summary

Dedicated teacher who builds engaging lessons and supports every learner.

Example achievement bullets

  • Taught 35 students per class and raised average test scores by 15%
  • Designed lesson plans aligned with national curriculum standards
  • Organised parent meetings and improved attendance by 10%

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
