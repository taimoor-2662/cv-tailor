How to Write a Teacher CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Schools look for qualifications, subjects and age groups first, then for evidence that students improved with you. This guide shows what to put in a teacher CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Lesson planning
  • Classroom management
  • Assessment
  • Curriculum development
  • Differentiated instruction
  • Parent communication
  • Edtech tools
  • Mentoring

Example professional summary

Dedicated teacher who builds engaging lessons and supports every learner.

Example achievement bullets

  • Taught 35 students per class and raised average test scores by 15%
  • Designed lesson plans aligned with national curriculum standards
  • Organised parent meetings and improved attendance by 10%

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a teacher CV

  • Leaving out your teaching qualification or registration.
  • Not stating the subjects, grade levels and class sizes.
  • Skipping classroom technology and assessment methods.

Tips that work for this role

  • Add results: test score improvements or attendance gains.
  • Mention extra-curricular activities, safeguarding training and mentoring.
