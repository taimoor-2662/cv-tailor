How to Write a Office Administrator CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a office administrator CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for Office AdministratorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Microsoft Office
  • Scheduling
  • Data entry
  • Filing and record keeping
  • Email management
  • Travel coordination
  • Customer service
  • Confidentiality

Example professional summary

Organised office administrator experienced in scheduling, record keeping and front-desk support.

Example achievement bullets

  • Managed daily office operations for a team of 15, cutting supply costs by 12%
  • Scheduled meetings and travel for 5 managers with zero conflicts
  • Maintained a digital filing system covering 2,000+ client records

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
