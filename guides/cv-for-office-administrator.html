How to Write an Office Administrator CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Office administrators keep a team running, so employers look for proof that you can organise people, paperwork and systems without close supervision. This guide shows what to put in an office administrator CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Microsoft Office
  • Scheduling
  • Data entry
  • Filing and record keeping
  • Email management
  • Travel coordination
  • Customer service
  • Confidentiality

Example professional summary

Organised office administrator experienced in scheduling, record keeping and front-desk support.

Example achievement bullets

  • Managed daily office operations for a team of 15, cutting supply costs by 12%
  • Scheduled meetings and travel for 5 managers with zero conflicts
  • Maintained a digital filing system covering 2,000+ client records

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in an office administrator CV

  • Writing 'answered phones and filed documents' without saying how many people or records you handled.
  • Leaving out software names such as Microsoft Excel, Outlook or Google Workspace.
  • Not mentioning confidentiality or the sensitive records you handled.

Tips that work for this role

  • Show scale: managers supported, meetings scheduled or invoices processed.
  • Mention one process you improved, such as a new filing or booking system.
