How to Write a Sales Executive CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Sales CVs are judged on results. A recruiter should see your targets and what you achieved against them within the first few lines. This guide shows what to put in a sales executive CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Lead generation
  • Negotiation
  • CRM
  • Cold calling
  • Closing deals
  • Target achievement
  • Client relationships
  • Market research

Example professional summary

Target-driven sales executive with a record of building client relationships and closing deals.

Example achievement bullets

  • Exceeded monthly sales targets by 18% for 6 consecutive months
  • Generated 60+ qualified leads per month through calls and networking
  • Closed deals worth over $250,000 in one year

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a sales executive CV

  • Writing 'target driven' without a single figure.
  • Not saying whether your sales were B2B or B2C, or the typical deal size.
  • Hiding your best result at the bottom of the page.

Tips that work for this role

  • Lead each role with your best number, such as quota attainment or revenue won.
  • Name the CRM and prospecting tools you used.
