How to Write a Fresh Graduate CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

With little work history, your CV should lead with education, projects, internships and skills employers can verify. This guide shows what to put in a fresh graduate CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Microsoft Office
  • Research
  • Time management
  • Adaptability
  • Relevant coursework
  • Internships

Example professional summary

Motivated graduate with relevant coursework, project experience and a strong willingness to learn.

Example achievement bullets

  • Completed a final-year project used by 3 local businesses
  • Led a 5-person team in a university competition and placed in the top 3
  • Completed a 3-month internship supporting daily office tasks

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a fresh graduate CV

  • Starting with a long objective instead of your degree and top skills.
  • Not describing projects, thesis work or internships with results.
  • Padding the CV with irrelevant school-level details.

Tips that work for this role

  • Put education first and add relevant coursework and your final-year project.
  • Include volunteering, societies and part-time jobs to show reliability.
