How to Write a Receptionist CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Reception is about first impressions. Employers want to see polish, reliability and the ability to juggle many small tasks. This guide shows what to put in a receptionist CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Front desk
  • Phone handling
  • Visitor management
  • Scheduling
  • Microsoft Office
  • Communication
  • Professional appearance
  • Multitasking

Example professional summary

Professional receptionist who makes a warm first impression and keeps the front desk running smoothly.

Example achievement bullets

  • Greeted and directed 100+ visitors daily
  • Managed a 10-line phone system and appointment calendar
  • Reduced visitor wait times by 20% with a new check-in process

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a receptionist CV

  • Listing 'good communication skills' without examples.
  • Leaving out the phone system, booking software or visitor system you used.
  • Not mentioning languages you speak.

Tips that work for this role

  • Add volume: calls per day or visitors handled.
  • Mention any process you improved, such as a new check-in method.
