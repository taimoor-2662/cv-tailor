How to Write a Receptionist CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a receptionist CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for ReceptionistCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Front desk
  • Phone handling
  • Visitor management
  • Scheduling
  • Microsoft Office
  • Communication
  • Professional appearance
  • Multitasking

Example professional summary

Professional receptionist who makes a warm first impression and keeps the front desk running smoothly.

Example achievement bullets

  • Greeted and directed 100+ visitors daily
  • Managed a 10-line phone system and appointment calendar
  • Reduced visitor wait times by 20% with a new check-in process

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
