How to Write a Graphic Designer CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

A design CV supports your portfolio. Keep it clean, readable and ATS-friendly, and link your best work. This guide shows what to put in a graphic designer CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Illustrator
  • Canva
  • Branding
  • Typography
  • Layout design
  • Social media graphics
  • Client communication

Example professional summary

Creative graphic designer delivering clear, on-brand visuals on tight deadlines.

Example achievement bullets

  • Designed branding for 20+ small businesses
  • Produced 100+ social media graphics per month
  • Increased client engagement by 25% with a new visual identity

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a graphic designer CV

  • Using a heavily designed CV that ATS software cannot read.
  • Listing software without examples of what you created.
  • Forgetting a portfolio link.

Tips that work for this role

  • Put your portfolio link at the top.
  • Describe the outcome of a project, like engagement or sales growth.
