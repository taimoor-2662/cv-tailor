How to Write a Graphic Designer CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a graphic designer CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for Graphic DesignerCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Illustrator
  • Canva
  • Branding
  • Typography
  • Layout design
  • Social media graphics
  • Client communication

Example professional summary

Creative graphic designer delivering clear, on-brand visuals on tight deadlines.

Example achievement bullets

  • Designed branding for 20+ small businesses
  • Produced 100+ social media graphics per month
  • Increased client engagement by 25% with new visual identity

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
