How to Write a CV for Dubai and Gulf Jobs

Gulf employers often expect a few extra details. Here is what to include.

  • Keep it to 1 to 2 pages with a clear, professional layout.
  • Include nationality, visa status and notice period if you have them.
  • Add languages you speak, such as English, Arabic and Urdu.
  • Show sales or performance numbers clearly, such as targets achieved.
  • A neat professional photo is common in the region but optional.
  • List local knowledge or licences if they are relevant, for example property regulations.
  • Check your ATS score and tailor your CV for each job.
