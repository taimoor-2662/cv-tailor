How to Write a Project Manager CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Project CVs should prove you deliver: on time, on budget and with happy stakeholders. This guide shows what to put in a project manager CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Project planning
  • Budgeting
  • Risk management
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Agile and Scrum
  • MS Project
  • Team leadership
  • Reporting

Example professional summary

Project manager who delivers on time and budget through clear planning and communication.

Example achievement bullets

  • Delivered 12 projects on time and 8% under budget
  • Led cross-functional teams of up to 20 people
  • Cut reporting time by 30% with a new tracking dashboard

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a project manager CV

  • Listing methodologies without projects that used them.
  • Not stating project size: budget, team and timeline.
  • Leaving out certifications such as PMP or PRINCE2.

Tips that work for this role

  • Put budget and team size in every project line.
  • Show how you handled risks or scope changes.
