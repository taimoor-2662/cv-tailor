How to Write a Customer Service Representative CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Hiring managers read these CVs for two things: how well you handle difficult conversations and the numbers that prove it. This guide shows what to put in a customer service representative CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Active listening
  • Conflict resolution
  • CRM software
  • Ticket handling
  • Phone and email support
  • Upselling
  • Product knowledge
  • Multitasking

Example professional summary

Friendly customer service representative who resolves issues quickly and keeps customers loyal.

Example achievement bullets

  • Resolved 40+ customer queries per day with a 95% satisfaction score
  • Reduced average response time by 20% through ticket prioritisation
  • Retained at-risk accounts by offering tailored solutions

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a customer service representative CV

  • Calling yourself a 'people person' with no satisfaction scores or resolution numbers.
  • Forgetting to name the channels you worked in: phone, email, chat or social media.
  • Leaving out the CRM or ticketing tools you used.

Tips that work for this role

  • Add CSAT, first-contact resolution or average handling time if you know them.
  • Give one example of turning an unhappy customer into a loyal one.
