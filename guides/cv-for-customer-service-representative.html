How to Write a Customer Service Representative CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Recruiters and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) look for specific skills and proof of results. This guide shows what to put in a customer service representative CV and gives examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Tailor my CV for Customer Service RepresentativeCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Active listening
  • Conflict resolution
  • CRM software
  • Ticket handling
  • Phone and email support
  • Upselling
  • Product knowledge
  • Multitasking

Example professional summary

Friendly customer service representative who resolves issues quickly and keeps customers loyal.

Example achievement bullets

  • Resolved 40+ customer queries per day with a 95% satisfaction score
  • Reduced average response time by 20% through ticket prioritisation
  • Retained at-risk accounts by offering tailored solutions

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Common mistakes

  • Listing duties instead of results.
  • Missing the keywords from the job description.
  • Using tables, icons or photos that ATS software cannot read.
  • Leaving out dates or contact details.
  • Sending the same CV to every employer.
