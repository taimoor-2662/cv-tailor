ATS-Friendly CV Checklist

Use this checklist before you send your CV to any employer.

  • Use a single column and standard fonts such as Arial or Calibri.
  • Use normal headings: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills.
  • Put your email, phone and city at the top, not in a header or image.
  • Include keywords from the job description, only for skills you really have.
  • Start bullets with action verbs and add numbers where you can.
  • Add dates (month and year) to every job and qualification.
  • Avoid tables, text boxes, icons and photos for ATS uploads.
  • Save as PDF unless the employer asks for Word.
