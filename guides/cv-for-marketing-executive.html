How to Write a Marketing Executive CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

Marketing CVs live on numbers. Show the channels you ran and what they delivered. This guide shows what to put in a marketing executive CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Social media marketing
  • SEO
  • Content creation
  • Email campaigns
  • Google Analytics
  • Campaign planning
  • Copywriting
  • Lead generation

Example professional summary

Marketing executive who plans data-driven campaigns that bring in leads.

Example achievement bullets

  • Grew social media followers by 60% in 6 months
  • Ran email campaigns with a 28% open rate
  • Generated 400+ leads through paid and organic campaigns

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in a marketing executive CV

  • Writing 'managed social media' without follower growth or engagement numbers.
  • Not naming the tools: Google Analytics, Meta Ads or an email platform.
  • Skipping budgets or lead numbers.

Tips that work for this role

  • Show growth: followers, leads, open rates or return on ad spend.
  • Include one campaign with its goal, your actions and the result.
