How to Write an HR Officer CV (ATS-Friendly Examples)

HR employers look for hands-on recruitment, records and policy experience, plus knowledge of local labour rules. This guide shows what to put in an hr officer CV, with examples you can adapt. Use only things that are true for you.

Use This Role in CV TailorCheck my ATS score

Keywords to include (if you have the skill)

  • Recruitment
  • Onboarding
  • Employee records
  • Payroll support
  • Labour law basics
  • Interviewing
  • HRIS
  • Employee relations

Example professional summary

HR officer experienced in recruitment, onboarding and employee support.

Example achievement bullets

  • Recruited 40+ employees across 6 departments in one year
  • Cut time-to-hire from 45 to 30 days
  • Maintained accurate records for 200+ staff

These are examples. Replace the numbers with your real results.

Mistakes to avoid in an hr officer CV

  • Not stating how many hires or employees you handled.
  • Skipping the HR systems you used, such as an HRIS or ATS.
  • Leaving out knowledge of local labour law and compliance.

Tips that work for this role

  • Show time-to-hire or retention improvements.
  • Mention confidentiality and any HR certification.
