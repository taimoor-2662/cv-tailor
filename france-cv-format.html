France / Belgium CV Format and Free CV Maker

French and Belgian CVs are short, with a clear headline stating the job you want.

Key rules for a French CV

  • Photo: Optional. Photos are common on French CVs but not required, and some employers prefer none.
  • Length: One page for most candidates; two pages for experienced professionals.
  • Personal details: Include contact details and city. Age and marital status are not necessary, and many modern CVs leave them out.

Sections to include

A title line (target job), professional experience, education and training, skills, languages, and optionally interests (centres d'interet).

Extra tips

  • Start with a headline naming the job you are applying for.
  • In Belgium, state your French, Dutch and English levels where relevant.

Common mistakes

  • Writing a CV longer than two pages.
  • Leaving out language levels in bilingual job markets.
