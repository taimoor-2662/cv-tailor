Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language summary of how the site works, not legal advice.

What you submit

When you upload or paste a CV and a job description, your file is read in your browser. The text is sent to our server function only to generate the result you asked for (CV, ATS tips, cover letter, translation, interview questions or LinkedIn text). We do not save it in a database and we do not keep a copy.

AI processing

The text is processed by Cloudflare Workers AI. Cloudflare states that it does not use customer content to train the AI models available on Workers AI or to improve its services, and that it does not store the content unless a storage product is used alongside it. This site does not use any storage product for your CV. Cloudflare, like any web host, still processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to run its network.

Some requests may also be processed by Google's Gemini API on a paid plan, where Google states it does not use prompts and responses to improve its products.

Your browser

Your photo, saved templates, job tracker entries and Design Studio drafts are stored only in your own browser and are never uploaded. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. A share link contains your CV text inside the web address, so anyone with the link can read it; share it only with people you trust.

Cookies, ads and partner links

The site may show partner (affiliate) links, which are clearly labelled and may earn us a commission at no cost to you. The site currently shows advertising; ad providers may use cookies to show and measure ads.

Accuracy

The AI can make mistakes. Please check names, dates and details before you send your CV to anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on our GitHub page.
