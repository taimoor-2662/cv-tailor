Chinese CVs are usually one clean page with a few personal details and clear achievements.

Key rules for a Chinese CV

  • Photo: Commonly included, especially for local employers. International employers may not require it.
  • Length: One page is the norm.
  • Personal details: Age, gender, hometown and contact details are traditionally listed; include them only if the employer expects them. Do not include ID numbers.

Sections to include

Basic information, education background, work experience, skills and certificates, and a self-evaluation.

Extra tips

  • Keep it to a single clean page and highlight measurable achievements.
  • Include language certificates such as CET-4/6, IELTS or TOEFL where relevant.

Common mistakes

  • Writing a long CV instead of one page.
  • Using a vague self-evaluation without examples.
