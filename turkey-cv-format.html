Turkey CV Format and Free CV Maker

Turkish CVs are short and usually include a few personal details alongside education and experience.

Key rules for a Turkish CV

  • Photo: Common. A neat professional photo is often included.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: Date of birth, marital status and, for male applicants, military service status are often listed by tradition; include them only if the employer expects them.

Sections to include

Personal information, education, work experience, skills, languages and references.

Extra tips

  • List foreign language levels clearly; English is a strong plus.
  • Recent graduates often list education before experience.

Common mistakes

  • Making the CV longer than two pages.
  • Leaving out language levels.
