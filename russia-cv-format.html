Key rules for a Eastern European CV

  • Photo: Often included in Russia and several Eastern European countries, but optional.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: Traditional CVs list date of birth and sometimes marital status; these are optional, and many modern employers do not need them.

Sections to include

Contact details, objective, work experience, education, skills, languages, and optional additional information.

Extra tips

  • Use one consistent date format and list the most recent job first.
  • Choose the local language as the CV language for local employers.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving out a clear objective or job title.
  • Mixing date formats.
