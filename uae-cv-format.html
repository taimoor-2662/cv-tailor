Key rules for a UAE CV

  • Photo: Common. A professional business-style photo is often included, but it is optional.
  • Length: Two pages is typical.
  • Personal details: Employers often expect nationality, visa status (for example visit, employment or residence visa), languages and notice period. Date of birth is sometimes listed. Do not include passport or Emirates ID numbers.

Sections to include

Profile, key skills, professional experience, education, certifications and licences, languages, and personal details.

Extra tips

  • Include relevant UAE registrations or licences, such as RERA broker registration for real estate roles.
  • State your driving licence (UAE or home country) if the job involves driving or client visits.

Common mistakes

  • Not stating your visa status and notice period.
  • Leaving out numbers such as sales targets and deal values.
