Key rules for a Southeast Asian CV

  • Photo: Varies. Singapore usually does not need one; the Philippines, Malaysia and Indonesia more often include one.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: In Singapore, state your citizenship or work-pass status. In some countries traditional CVs list date of birth, civil status and expected salary. Include these only if asked.

Sections to include

Profile or objective, work experience, education, skills, languages, and personal particulars if required.

Extra tips

  • Mention notice period and expected salary only when the advert asks.
  • English is common in business; add other languages if useful.

Common mistakes

  • Listing salary expectations when they were not requested.
  • Not stating your work-pass or citizenship status in Singapore.
