Key rules for a Saudi, Qatar and Kuwait CV

  • Photo: Often included; keep it professional. It is generally optional.
  • Length: Two pages is typical.
  • Personal details: State nationality and residence or work-permit status clearly (for example the iqama in Saudi Arabia). Notice period and driving licence are commonly listed. Do not include ID or passport numbers.

Sections to include

Profile, experience, education, certifications, languages, and personal details.

Extra tips

  • Saudi employers must meet nationalisation (Saudization) targets, so nationality is a key filter; state it clearly.
  • Mention Arabic and English levels. Attested qualifications may be needed later.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving nationality or residence status unclear.
  • Not mentioning Arabic ability where it is relevant.
