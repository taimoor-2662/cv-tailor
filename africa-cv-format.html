Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa) CV Format and Free CV Maker

In Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa a CV is usually two pages with referees listed.

Key rules for a African CV

  • Photo: Usually not required. Some employers and traditional formats include one, so follow the advert.
  • Length: Two pages, up to three for senior roles.
  • Personal details: Traditional formats list date of birth, marital status and state of origin. Modern employers do not require them; include only what is asked. Do not include national ID numbers.

Sections to include

Career profile, key skills, work experience, education and qualifications, professional memberships, and referees.

Extra tips

  • Include two or three referees with job titles and contact details, or state that they are available on request.
  • List professional body memberships, such as ICAN in Nigeria or SAICA in South Africa, if relevant.

Common mistakes

  • Listing referees who have not agreed to be contacted.
  • Using a long personal-details block at the top.
