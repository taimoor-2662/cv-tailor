South Asia (Pakistan, India, Bangladesh) CV Format and Free CV Maker

CVs in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh follow similar conventions: a career objective, qualifications and a closing declaration. This page focuses on Pakistan and applies broadly to South Asia.

Key rules for a Pakistani and South Asian CV

  • Photo: Common in Pakistan and Bangladesh, and optional for many corporate roles in India. Use a clear, professional photo if you include one.
  • Length: One to two pages.
  • Personal details: Traditional CVs list father's name, date of birth, nationality and marital status. Many modern employers do not need them, so include only what the job asks. Never put your CNIC, Aadhaar, passport or bank details on a CV.

Sections to include

Career objective or profile, professional experience, academic qualifications, skills, certifications, languages, and a declaration line.

Extra tips

  • End with a short declaration that the information is true, which is a common local convention.
  • For jobs abroad (UAE, UK, Europe) switch to that region's format.

Common mistakes

  • Including ID card numbers or a full home address.
  • Using a vague objective copied from a template.
